1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how FuelBolivia accesses, uses, transmits, stores, and protects data when users view fuel-station information, use location-based features, submit community reports, or use authorized operator or administrator access.

2. Location data

With device permission, FuelBolivia can access approximate and precise device location. Location is used for functions such as calculating distance to nearby fuel stations and supporting proximity-dependent reporting features.

Location-related requests are transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections when the relevant online feature requires backend processing.

3. Operator and administrator authentication

FuelBolivia supports sign-in for authorized station operators and administrators. For these accounts, email addresses and authentication/session information are processed for login, session management, password recovery, and authorization of restricted functions.

The current Android app does not provide public self-service account creation for ordinary users. Authorized operator or administrator accounts are provisioned separately.

4. Community and station reports

FuelBolivia may process community or station-status reports, such as queue information, estimated waiting time, vehicle count, confirmations, and related station information, in order to display current fuel-station conditions to users.

5. Service providers and sharing

KOPPSUISSE S.R.L. does not sell personal data.

FuelBolivia uses third-party service providers where necessary to operate the application:

  • Supabase: backend database, authentication, and related infrastructure services.
  • OpenStreetMap / osmdroid: map data and map rendering functionality.

Data may be transmitted to these service providers only as required to provide the relevant application functionality.

6. Security

Network communication between the app and online services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Client access uses publishable client credentials and authenticated user session tokens where authentication is required. Restricted backend access is controlled by server-side authorization policies.

7. Data retention

Operator or administrator account data is retained while authorized access remains active and for as long as necessary to administer that access, protect the service, and meet legitimate legal or security obligations.

Community and station-status information may be retained while it remains relevant to the operation, integrity, and history of the service. Information that no longer needs to identify an individual may be anonymized, aggregated, or deleted.

8. Deletion and privacy requests

You may request access to or deletion of personal data associated with FuelBolivia by contacting jluzcuber@gmail.com. Authorized operator and administrator account holders may also request deletion of their account-linked personal data.

For instructions, visit the FuelBolivia Account and Data Deletion page.

When a valid deletion request is completed, account-linked personal data is deleted unless limited retention is required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, operational, or legal obligations.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FuelBolivia functionality or data practices change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.

FuelBolivia is an independent application published by KOPPSUISSE S.R.L. and is not affiliated with or representing any government entity.

Request deletion

FuelBolivia currently provides authenticated access for authorized station operators and administrators. Public self-service account creation is not currently offered in the Android app.

If you have an authorized FuelBolivia operator or administrator account, or if you want to request deletion of personal data associated with your use of FuelBolivia, send a request to jluzcuber@gmail.com with the subject FuelBolivia Data Deletion Request.

Information to include

  • The email address associated with the operator or administrator account, if applicable.
  • A short description of the data or account you want deleted.
  • Any information reasonably necessary for us to verify that the request relates to you. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other secrets.

What happens after a valid request

After verifying the request, KOPPSUISSE S.R.L. will delete the account-linked personal data that is no longer required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, operational, or legal obligations. Where a service provider processes the relevant data on our behalf, we will take the necessary steps to remove the data from that service as applicable.

Community or station-status reports that are not personal data may be retained in anonymized or non-identifying form when needed to preserve the integrity and historical operation of the service.

Retention

Account information is retained while authorized access is active and for as long as necessary to administer that access. Personal data connected to a valid deletion request is removed once the request has been verified and processed, except where limited retention is required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.
